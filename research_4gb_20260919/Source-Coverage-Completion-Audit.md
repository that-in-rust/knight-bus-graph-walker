## What Final Reading Changed

- It rejected the premise that Neo4j/GDS necessarily stores every edge as an inefficient JVM object; compressed and optional packed representations are relevant comparators.
- It separated stored-data capacity, projected topology, algorithm state, preparation peaks and output. Disk-backed storage alone does not establish bounded analytics.
- It preserved the actual narrow Bolt proof while refusing to transplant its results into full PageRank, huge outputs or a source-to-answer 4 GB claim.
- It exposed repeated anecdotes, index omissions, SKU/unit mismatches and version-specific defects. Neither isolated complaints nor hypothetical list-price arithmetic validates willingness to pay or a whole market.
- It shifted the product from a kernel benchmark toward first-answer, repeated-use and refresh economics, without making all seventeen research options prerequisites for the first useful workflow.
