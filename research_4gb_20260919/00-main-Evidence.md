## Source-Specific Interpretation

### A007: Preserve The User Job, Strengthen The Success Gate

The document's first customer owns a graph artifact and a high-stakes question blocked by uncertain execution. Its first-priority algorithm set is traversal, WCC, then centrality; similarity and community detection follow. This is a product hypothesis supported by public pain anecdotes, not evidence of paid demand. Artifact ingestion failures in BloodHound-like workflows may have little to do with algorithm kernels, so build/export time belongs in every comparison.

One acceptance criterion is too weak: a prototype that either refuses or stays under budget can pass by refusing all useful work. Our study needs nontrivial completed workloads on the 4 GB deployment and a specified time envelope, alongside correctness and resource bounds. A007's own distinction between a technical milestone and market validation supports this stronger gate.

### Everything-We-Know-Now: Useful Contradictions Across Time

The document corrects earlier claims about empty competition and absent estimators. Its next leap, that an in-process Neo4j plugin is uniquely protected from competitors, does not follow: competing engines can also be wrapped by procedures or services. A native library inside the database process also shares that process's failure domain. A separate bounded worker with a thin procedure adapter is a design candidate, but it still pays extraction, source-consistency, serialization, and deployment costs.

This creates two distinct product scenarios to retain, rather than silently choosing one:

1. A 4 GB standalone artifact runner; source extraction may happen elsewhere and must be reported.
2. A worker integrated with an existing Neo4j deployment; 4 GB limits the added worker, not the combined Neo4j host. Database/source RAM is additional.

The document's time-sensitive prices, competitor capabilities, and absence-of-feature assertions are historical claims as of July 2026 until freshly verified.

### Arch05: Predictable Allocation Is Stronger Than Predicting Every Workload

An exact, small metadata summary cannot generally determine topology-sensitive runtime, frontier expansion, triangle counts, or candidate overlap. Counts/degrees can bound some work, and implementation-fixed arenas can bound RAM. These are different forms of evidence.

A safer proposed improvement is to compile an explicit bounded execution schedule: allocated buffers, maximum decoded block size, active state windows, result streaming, spill-run fan-in, and file lifetimes. The plan supplies a checked allocation ceiling; runtime and spill-size predictions retain ranges or conservative bounds. This is not yet a claim of global novelty: external-memory schedules and static memory planning are established concepts.

The phrase "KB, never larger" needs qualification: metadata proportional to all blocks, degree-CDF knots, or file entries grows with the graph. Use a bounded resident directory window over disk-resident metadata, or give the exact metadata size.

### Conclusion V2: Corrections Required Before Reusing Numbers

1. Two f32 PageRank vectors at V=200M use 1.6 billion bytes. That is a representation size, not a universal lower bound on resident memory. Vectors can be external, tiled, compressed where data permits, or computed with a different update schedule. Each alternative changes I/O, semantics, or both and must be explicit.
2. Comparing f32 ranks to an f64 baseline is numerical compatibility under a stated tolerance, not bitwise exactness.
3. Reusing a computed answer moves work before the request. It does not establish an asymptotic exponent improvement in the original end-to-end computation, particularly when E scales with V and iteration count is fixed. Compare both systems with equivalent result reuse.
4. A hardware traffic estimate is a conditional floor or model, not a portable measurement. An LDBC run scaled linearly by edge count does not establish the speed of a different graph, configuration, iteration rule, or machine.
5. Putting reciprocal degree on every edge costs extra disk traffic and does not automatically halve the dominant rank gather. A normalized source-contribution vector computed once per iteration is an alternative to evaluate.
6. A top-K result must be compared against top-K on the incumbent, not against serialization of every incumbent result row.

### Arch06: Promising Levers Need Composition Costs

The document already includes compressed frontiers, quantized state, sketch-assisted similarity, hot/cold state, direction switching, delta iteration, reordering, prefetch, arenas, and memoized results. These cannot be presented as new discoveries in this study.

Important constraints: a small change in input can cause a large connected-component change; deletes can split giant components; dropping a locally small PageRank update needs a global residual/error argument; exact reranking cannot recover candidates omitted by a sketch; a capped community tally can change the Louvain decision. Streaming edges also does not make vertex arrays fit by itself.

## Executed Analytical Checks

On 2026-09-19, a small Node.js calculation constructed two explicit 12-vertex undirected graphs, counted edges/degrees/triangles by enumeration, and ran ordinary BFS from vertex zero to depth six. It produced:

| Graph | V | E | All vertex degrees | Reachable within 6 hops, including seed | Triangles |
| --- | ---: | ---: | --- | ---: | ---: |
| A: cycle C12 | 12 | 12 | 2 | 12 | 0 |
| B: four disjoint triangles | 12 | 12 | 2 | 3 | 4 |

Both graphs have identical node count, edge count, and exact degree distribution. Those metadata alone do not determine traversal expansion or motif work. This does not refute every possible richer manifest; it refutes the use of those summary fields alone as an exact topology-dependent predictor.

The same calculation checked representation sizes:

| V | Two f32 vectors | Two f64 vectors | One visited bitmap |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| 200M | 1,600,000,000 B | 3,200,000,000 B | 25,000,000 B |
| 1B | 8,000,000,000 B | 16,000,000,000 B | 125,000,000 B |

Against our provisional 3,000,000,000-byte worker budget, the 200M-node pair of f64 vectors already exceeds the allowance before other working state. These are arithmetic checks, not performance benchmarks.

2. The sharper opportunities concern avoiding work within those structures: certify when an old answer remains exact at a new snapshot; execute repeated topology patterns without expanding each edge; compute a similarity score once for an equivalence class while preserving per-entity output semantics.
3. Fresh primary search immediately found compressed adjacency-matrix multiplication/PageRank antecedents (arXiv:1708.07271 and the 2022 journal version). Hence the proposed row-factoring plan is an adaptation and integration hypothesis, not an original PageRank algorithm.
