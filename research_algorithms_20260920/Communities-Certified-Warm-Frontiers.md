# Certified Warm-State Community Frontiers

Date: 2026-09-21. A04 research continuation. Current status: implemented
certificate and stream prototype, exact finite oracle checks passed;
independent mathematical challenge completed and lead-replayed. This is a broader input
certificate for the existing monotone-stream reduction, not general Louvain,
not a claim of publication priority, and not yet a measured RAM improvement.

## Goal And Alternatives

**Goal:** preserve the complete serial local-moving trajectory from a supplied
two-community warm state while eliminating resident per-vertex mutable labels
in an eligible execution phase. Account for certificate construction separately.

**Architecture:** certify a weighted graph and its warm partition, reduce the
eligible active region to two immutable mate streams, and emit exact moves and
labels with scalar control state. Anchor adjacency need not be dense, regular,
or generated by the old fixed template.

**Tech stack:** exact Python rational arithmetic for falsification; streaming
interfaces for execution-state accounting. No native performance claim.

Expert lenses: serial semantics, external-memory accounting, sufficient
certificates, and adversarial validation. Considered alternatives:

1. Keep the fixed dense template and improve its constants: does not broaden
   eligibility, so not selected.
2. Build a general external priority-queue local mover: broader coverage but
   already has substantial prior art and loses the scan-only invariant.
3. Certify a broader warm-state class: selected. It gains sparse and irregular
   anchor graphs, but retains uniform mover affinities, matching-only mover
   interaction, and a prefix-separated visit order.
4. Treat stability as proof-carrying preprocessing: the graph producer may
   supply a candidate partition, but the builder must independently check it.
   This is combined with option 3, not permission to trust a guessed partition.

## Exact Contract

The source is a simple undirected loopless graph with positive rational stored
weights (absent edges have zero weight). Resolution gamma is positive rational.
Vertices have unique integer IDs. Partition all vertices into nonempty anchors
P0, nonempty anchors Q0, and an even-sized mover set Y with size b>=2.

All P0 vertices initially carry token P; all Q0 carry Q. In increasing Y ID
order, the first k movers carry Q and the rest P. Put r0=k+1 and require
1<=r0<=b-1. Anchor IDs may be interleaved with movers. A full sweep visits all
original IDs increasingly; accept only strictly positive modularity gain,
stay on zero gain, consider P, Q and a fresh singleton destination, and stop
after a full no-move sweep. There is no contraction/refinement phase.

Y-Y adjacency must be exactly a reciprocal perfect matching of common weight
w>0. Each mover has the same total affinity alpha to P0 and beta to Q0,
hence common weighted degree d=alpha+beta+w. Neither individual anchor degrees
nor the internal anchor graphs must be uniform. P0-Q0 edges are permitted.
The proposed certificate checks all these facts from the actual graph.

This starts at the declared warm state. It does NOT show how a singleton-start
run discovers the anchors, or that an arbitrary production partition qualifies.

## Scalar Reduction

Let T=sum(degree)=2m, where m is total edge weight, and use M for the number
of stored undirected edges. Let Ap=sum(degree(P0)), Aq=sum(degree(Q0)). If r movers
currently belong to Q, define

```text
VP(r) = Ap + d*(b-r)             VQ(r) = Aq + d*r
h     = gamma*d*d
F(r)  = T*(beta-alpha) - gamma*d*(Aq-Ap-d*b) - 2*h*r
a     = T*w-h                    B = T*w+h
f0    = F(r0)
```

For a vertex u moving from C to D, define the exact scaled gain

```text
G = T*(weight(u,D)-weight(u,C without u))
    - gamma*degree(u)*(volume(D)-volume(C)+degree(u)).
actual modularity change = 2*G / T^2.
```

For a split matching pair, its P endpoint has gain F(r)+a and its Q endpoint
a-F(r). A same-P pair endpoint has gain F(r)-B; a same-Q endpoint has
-F(r)-B. Require the strict band condition

```text
abs(f0) < a < 2*h-abs(f0).
```

At r=r0-1 only split P endpoints move, at r0 both split endpoints move, and
at r0+1 only split Q endpoints move. Also abs(F(r))<B at these three states,
so same-side and already reunited pairs stay fixed. This is a sufficient
certificate, not a necessary characterization of all streamable trajectories.

## Preventing A Third Community

For every r in {r0-1,r0,r0+1}, require

```text
-T*alpha + gamma*d*(VP(r)-d) <= 0
-T*beta  + gamma*d*(VQ(r)-d) <= 0.
```

These conservatively ignore any source-side mate affinity. They bound the
fresh-singleton gain for a mover on either side. They prevent an unnoticed
third destination from invalidating the two-token recurrence.

## Anchor Stability Certificate

For an anchor u, let du be its degree, Ip its affinity to P0, Iq its affinity
to Q0, and W its total mover affinity. Let top_u(t) be the sum of its t largest
weights to Y, including b implicit zero positions. An implementation only
needs to sort nonzero weights, not expand all the zeros. The minimum sum of
any t incident mover positions is W-top_u(b-t).

For a P0 anchor, test at each of the three r values:

```text
own_min = Ip + W - top_u(r)
T*(Iq-Ip-W+2*top_u(r)) - gamma*du*(VQ(r)-VP(r)+du) <= 0
-T*own_min + gamma*du*(VP(r)-du) <= 0.
```

For a Q0 anchor:

```text
own_min = Iq + W - top_u(b-r)
T*(Ip-Iq-W+2*top_u(b-r)) - gamma*du*(VP(r)-VQ(r)+du) <= 0
-T*own_min + gamma*du*(VQ(r)-du) <= 0.
```

These cover EVERY subset of r movers assigned to Q, including configurations
the actual trace never visits. The bounds can reject a genuinely stable graph;
they must not accept an unstable one. By induction, anchors stay, no fresh
community appears, the scalar volumes are correct, and the band remains closed.

## Exact Schedule And Output

Let c be the number of matching pairs crossing the initial prefix boundary.
Accepted endpoint sides are the length-c prefix of H,H,L,L,H,H,L,L,...,
where H is the least unresolved high-ID/P mover and L the least unresolved
low-ID/Q mover. Anchor no-ops do not affect this relative order.

For zero-based accepted completion j, sweep=1+floor((j+2)/4). When c>0,
total sweeps including the final no-move sweep=2+floor((c+1)/4); for c=0,
total sweeps=1. Unlike the old template, this certificate does not assume c
odd. Final r for c modulo four 0,1,2,3 is r0-1,r0,r0+1,r0 respectively.

The two-frontier lemma in [the original manuscript](Communities-Monotone-Frontier-Streams.md)
applies unchanged. Store each mover's original ID and its mate's Y rank.
A pair is unresolved exactly when both ranks are at or beyond the consumed
frontiers. Selected H assigns its pair Q; selected L assigns P. Later stale
counterparts infer their label from the consuming side. Fixed same-side pairs
keep their initial label. Emit two sorted mover-label streams; merge these
with the sorted anchor-label stream for complete original-ID output.

```text
Graph + warm labels
        |
        v
Paid certificate construction -- reject --> ordinary solver / explanation
        |
        v
Scalar header + two mate streams + anchor labels
        |
        v
Two monotone frontiers -> chronological move log
        |
        v
Sorted label spools -> merge -> complete original-ID labels
```

Execution is O(n) exact-arithmetic operations with constant control records
and a bounded number of buffers. For fixed-width records, transfers are
O(Scan(n)); for variable-width IDs or rationals, account for serialized size S
and O(Scan(S)) transfers as well as actual arithmetic/comparison bit costs.
Unique integer IDs are not automatically O(log n)-bit machine words. Bounded
widths and arithmetic intermediates require range proofs in a native build.
Prepared input and full output remain O(n) records, not necessarily O(n)
fixed-width words. No claim that the Python builder or collecting callbacks
are constant RAM. The builder stores O(n+M) records; external construction is future
work requiring paid sorting/grouping and stream validation.

The output contract is accepted moves (optionally with exact gains), logical
sweep numbers and every final label. It does not include a materialized event
for every no-op vertex visit: that expanded transcript has n times the sweep
count records and can be Theta(n*c). The scheduler can emit decisions without
rational arithmetic after admission; the current gain-reporting kernel retains
exact rational arithmetic costs.

## Sparse Candidate Family To Falsify

For b>=16 divisible by eight, take |P0|=2b, |Q0|=b, |Y|=b, gamma=1/2,
k=3b/4-1. Replace the old P clique by a Hamiltonian cycle with each edge weight
11*(2b-1)/2 and the Q clique by a cycle with edge weight 11*(b-1)/2.
Attach each mover to two unique P anchors with weights 3b, partitioning P0;
attach each mover to one unique Q anchor with weight 4b. Add arbitrary unit
perfect matching on Y. Cycle orders and assignments can be permuted.

This has 4b vertices and 13b/2 stored undirected edges, preserving the old
aggregate degrees and band scalars. Anchor stability still needs checking;
aggregate equality alone does not prove it. Perturb anchor edges rationally to
test genuinely irregular accepted interiors rather than one regular cycle.
All such examples are constructed, not representative customer workloads.

## Prior Art And Scope Of Possible Contribution

[VLouvain, EDBT 2026](https://www.openproceedings.org/2026/conf/edbt/paper-72.pdf)
already computes modularity updates from aggregated vectors without explicitly
constructing low-rank graph edges. Algebraic affinity aggregation is therefore
not our novelty claim. Our proposed distinction is a verifiable warm-state
reduction to forward-only trace/output streams, with sparse irregular anchor
graphs as a possible admitted class. This difference still needs an exhaustive
closest-art check; sparse support by itself is not enough for publication.

[Smart local moving](https://arxiv.org/abs/1308.6604) and
[Louvain to Leiden](https://arxiv.org/abs/1810.08473) address broader algorithmic
pipelines. We do not inherit their quality guarantees, refinement phases or
performance by preserving one deliberately specified serial local-moving pass.

## Paid External Preparation: Design, Not Implementation

The current Python builder is resident. A possible external construction must
perform these actual data operations, not assume a pre-certified mate file:

1. Validate and sort the supplied vertex roles and initial labels. Assign Y
   ranks in original-ID order; verify the Q-prefix/P-suffix property.
2. Canonicalize and validate source edges, rejecting loops, invalid endpoints
   and duplicate simple-graph edges. Join both endpoints to the role/rank file.
3. Group endpoint records to derive degrees, anchor affinities and matching
   degrees. Verify each mover has one Y neighbor, common w/alpha/beta, and
   derive T, Ap, Aq and the strict-band scalars. Reciprocity follows from a
   validated undirected source; do not infer it from local range checks alone.
4. Group anchor-to-Y weights by anchor and descending weight. For each anchor,
   fold only the required three top-r positions (or b-r positions) plus totals.
   Combine with degree/anchor affinities and evaluate all six inequalities.
5. Emit sorted low/high mate records and anchor labels, tied to source and
   warm-state identity. Publish only after all certificate checks succeed.

For fixed-width, range-checked records these operations suggest O(Sort(n+M))
transfers and O(n+M) retained/temporary record scale using an external sorter;
that is a design-level bound, not a delivered implementation or a measured
peak scratch bound. Constants, simultaneous intermediate files, merge fan-in,
sort RAM, record width and integer overflow policy need an executable budget.
The source graph, prepared files, result spools and move log must be included
in an end-to-end deployment ledger. Query-time label-state savings alone do
not pay back preparation when a user runs only one small job.

## Next Structural Question

Exact uniform mover affinities are still a brittle eligibility gate. A more
useful follow-on would certify only the required gain SIGNS for every
reachable band configuration, allowing different mover affinities, degrees
and matching weights. This is a new unimplemented direction, not a result
covered by the 150-case receipt or the completed review.

For a mover u with matched vertex v, condition on their P/Q memberships and
on r. The Q-volume is Aq plus a degree sum over a cardinality-constrained
subset of Y. Its minimum/maximum can be bounded using degree order statistics
with u and v excluded, plus the degrees of endpoints forced into Q. The
modularity gain is affine in that volume. If every high endpoint is strictly
eligible/blocked according to the same three-state table, every low endpoint
obeys its corresponding table, reunited pairs stay and fresh/anchor moves
are excluded, the HHLL order argument should not require identical gains.

A future stream would carry each mover's degree/affinities/matching weight
and update one exact Q-volume scalar on each accepted move. Its exact gains
would no longer be reconstructed from one common F(r). The decisive tests
are asymmetric matching pairs and perturbations that defeat the current
uniform-signature check, compared against the complete expanded oracle.
Do not implement a robust-looking tolerance test without those worst-case
membership bounds: differing degrees can otherwise change move order.

## Independent Challenge And Corrections

The [separate review](Communities-Certified-Warm-Review.md) uses no lead code
or fixtures. The lead inspected its checker and replayed it successfully with
Python 3.11 and warnings as errors. Counts: 3,744 exact modularity differences,
55,980 anchor subsets, 14,202 anchor-extremum checks, 344 strict-band cases,
27,975 scalar schedule replays, and 143 expanded admitted graphs. The expanded
graphs include 24 zero-crossing, 48 positive-even-crossing, 93 interleaved-ID
and 50 irregular-anchor cases. Three rejected witnesses show why dropping
singleton/anchor gates or making the lower band inequality non-strict is unsafe.

Corrections integrated from review:

- State resource bounds in records/serialized bytes unless widths are bounded.
- Charge exact numerical gains separately from the direction-only scheduler.
- Do not imply that all no-op visit events can be emitted in linear work.
- Distinguish total edge weight m from stored edge count M in builder costs.
- Require prepared streams to belong to the certified source/partition snapshot.

The reviewer also observes that the UPPER strict inequality can safely be
relaxed to a<=2h-abs(f0) because zero-gain moves stay. The implementation keeps
the stated stricter sufficient certificate; no extra inputs are claimed. The
LOWER inequality abs(f0)<a must remain strict for this HHLL theorem.

Additional [lead regressions](experiments/test_certified_warm_review_contracts.py)
exercise all fifteen allowed prefix sizes on two differently permuted sparse
graphs by adjusting anchor volumes, reject the lower-band equality witness
and the isolated-anchor singleton witness, and replay with 513-bit IDs.
The four review-driven tests add to ten original new tests and six untouched
old frontier tests: 20 passing tests. The original 150-case receipt and its
source hashes remain unchanged. This closes the bounded theorem-review step,
not the seven-family contribution or paper-readiness gates.

## Concrete Heterogeneous Follow-On Bounds: Not Yet Implemented

This section makes the next experiment executable as a research question.
It is not part of the reviewed uniform-affinity theorem or its measurements.
Retain two anchors, a perfect mover matching, prefix-separated labels and
three reachable cardinalities, but permit distinct du, alpha_u, beta_u and
matching weights w_uv. Let DQ be the sum of mover degrees currently in Q.
Then VQ=Aq+DQ and VP=T-VQ; a streamed move can update DQ by adding or
subtracting only the degree in the current record.

For each mover u and its mate v, condition on whether each is in Q. Write
i_u,i_v in {0,1}. At cardinality r, the remaining t=r-i_u-i_v Q movers come
from Y minus {u,v}. If t is outside [0,b-2], this configuration is impossible
and imposes no inequality. Otherwise calculate

```text
zmin = Aq + i_u*du + i_v*dv + sum_of_t_smallest_other_degrees
zmax = Aq + i_u*du + i_v*dv + sum_of_t_largest_other_degrees.
```

For u in P, put e=beta_u-alpha_u+(w_uv if v in Q else -w_uv):

```text
switch_min = T*e - gamma*du*(2*zmax-T+du)
switch_max = T*e - gamma*du*(2*zmin-T+du)
fresh_max  = -T*(alpha_u + (w_uv if v in P else 0))
             + gamma*du*(T-zmin-du).
```

For u in Q, put e=alpha_u-beta_u+(w_uv if v in P else -w_uv):

```text
switch_min = T*e - gamma*du*(T-2*zmin+du)
switch_max = T*e - gamma*du*(T-2*zmax+du)
fresh_max  = -T*(beta_u + (w_uv if v in Q else 0))
             + gamma*du*(zmax-du).
```

Require fresh_max<=0 everywhere. For a same-side mate require switch_max<=0.
For split pairs at r0-1 require P switch_min>0 and Q switch_max<=0; at r0
require both switch_min>0; at r0+1 require Q switch_min>0 and P switch_max<=0.
These are sufficient over all subsets consistent with the endpoint labels,
not just observed execution. The HHLL proof depends on those signs and visit
order, not equal numerical gains. A new implementation must still verify
these inequalities against an independent oracle before promoting the claim.

For an anchor, use the global r-smallest/r-largest mover degree sums to bound
VQ. Combine its existing top-r affinity maximum with the appropriate volume
extreme: P-switch and P-fresh maxima use VQ_min; Q-switch and Q-fresh maxima
use VQ_max. Affinity and degree extrema may describe different subsets, making
this combined anchor bound conservative rather than exact. Ignoring that
correlation cannot cause unsafe admission if both terms are maximized in
the correct direction; it can cause refusal.

After one degree sort and prefix-sum construction, removing two endpoints
requires adjusting the selected prefix/suffix by at most two positions per
cardinality query. This suggests O(b log b) degree preprocessing and O(b)
such constant-size queries, rather than enumerating exponentially many mover
subsets. Anchor weight sorting is still paid independently. This is familiar
order-statistic machinery; any proposed contribution is the exact certified
trajectory reduction, not sorting or interval arithmetic itself.

Historical next test sequence: first verify subset extrema by exhaustive tiny enumeration;
then perturb each matching pair and mover-anchor edge independently, checking
that the old uniform certifier refuses while the new one admits only when
the full expanded serial oracle agrees. Retain safe refusals and adversaries
where mixed degrees reverse a move. Preserve source/partition discovery and
whole-lifecycle RAM as separate unsolved obligations.
