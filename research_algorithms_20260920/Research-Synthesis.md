## The Direct Assessment

There are now seven concrete algorithm candidates, not merely seven storage-format names. Their proposed contributions have different strengths:

- Paths fits the dependency/security workflow, but its original quotient exactly equals single-predecessor elimination. The new [access frontier](Paths-Access-Frontier.md) survived independent correctness review, with two safe strengthenings. A factored transit-access competitor matches its stated static and changed-metric family; a more general dynamic-range contribution remains open.
- Community detection's serial positive-move certificate now has a [complete singleton-start family](Communities-Natural-Crossover.md) with a later partial moving run. Its whole-trajectory score-update savings are real in the named cached engine, but generic quadratic line search matches it. There is still no multi-vertex cohort in the singleton first sweep or established novel algorithmic separation.
- PageRank now has a stronger state/contraction tradeoff: heavy diagonal release gives at most 2r abstract factors and beta<=2a/(1+a). Independent review strengthened the selected-vertex bound to r. A positive selected-factor kernel avoids expanding restored rows; its scalar payload bound is 10r+4, excluding named metadata/numerical/runtime costs. Release can still lose even after a failed initial certificate, so it is a plan option, not a universally faster solver.
- The subsequent [release staircase](PageRank-Release-Staircase.md) supplies integer tiers: at most (m+1)r factors and contraction a(m+1)/(m+a), with a sharp constrained-factorization family and at-most-mr candidate stream. Its exact probe and independent mathematical challenge passed; numerical threshold and canonical-record requirements are now explicit.
- New [anisotropic/mixed release](PageRank-Anisotropic-Release.md) exploits pointwise factor mixtures and fixed-set orientation. A cut minimizes one survival objective, but can miss the minimum buffer reservation. The [exact factor-conditioned planner](PageRank-Factor-Conditioned-Planner.md) resolves that named objective through at most 3^g disappearance-witness assignments, with an exact cardinality envelope; g is the number of killable supplied factors. This is a proved specialization with finite oracle evidence, not yet new generic optimization or a practical PageRank speedup.
- The [natural PageRank comparison](PageRank-Native-Incidence-Evidence.md) supplies eight solvers and actual rounded-output interval certification on native memberships. Its original 72 workers show reduced power losing by 4.81x to fused ordinary power. The now-reviewed and implemented [mass correction](PageRank-Mass-Correction-Experiment.md) reduces checks from 108-116 to 17-18 but only matches ordinary power's time. In 24 locally stable pairs out of 27, median candidate/control ratios are 1.010 for correction, 0.781 for established factor CG and 0.451 for paid reused-dense solving. All 243 outputs are certified; dense uses about 55 MB worker RSS versus overlapping non-dense ranges around 19-20 MB. The subsequent [scalar publication experiment](PageRank-Streaming-Certificate-Experiment.md) implements a reviewed ledger with a stronger published summation control, 960 exact-output checks and 81 independently audited public outputs. It halves modeled publisher reads but is about 13% slower than the strongest direct path, with overlapping RSS. Neither study establishes a new solver, complete-process RAM improvement or seven-family novelty.
- Connectivity now adds optional O(tree cuts + live insertions) active fragment state and a [watched-debit schedule](Connectivity-Watched-Debits.md) that can enable certificates as actual connectivity grows. It keeps only enough crossing failures to establish each unsafe guard. Forest fragments, watched constraints and strong certificates are known; the graph-specific adaptive schedule and full-cost advantage remain under investigation.
- Similarity has three storage/query options: a compact outsider envelope may reject; paid exception merge retains complete per-anchor buckets; [runwise shared selection](Similarity-Runwise-Selection.md) instead retains one query-independent disk RMQ and consumes interval postings. The last can avoid expanding q=n changed targets when the overlap field has few runs. Bounded seeds reduce selector state to O(k), ordered merging avoids event sorting, and [strict run bounds](Similarity-Run-Bound-Pruning.md) skip some initial RMQs. The latest ordinary DAAT control beats the pruned route on all 30 sampled public sources, despite its gains over full scanning. Scientific differentiation and a useful compressed domain remain unresolved.
- Triangles now include a [voltage-cover alternative](Voltage-Cover-Triangles.md) with a connected, twin-free representation-size separation from uniform-block storage. It can return a compact functional local-count answer without expanding fibres. Voltage-aware IVM matches the bounds; full numeric output still grows with the actual vertex count.
- Embeddings has three numerical/work routes: exact grouped completion may require large common denominators; [lattice sum replay](Embeddings-Lattice-History-Replay.md) removes Q^L width; [compact mean replay](Embeddings-Mean-History-Replay.md) also removes feature-cardinality dependence from retained scalar ranges at a certified precision cost. The latter two retain quadratic-depth replay. A new observed-defect gate certifies some exact coarse-grid outputs that worst-case bounds refuse. The [packed experiment](Embeddings-Packed-Workflow-Evidence.md) now supplies real restricted source-to-output measurements: in-place arena conversion removes the earlier conversion-file cost, but the fused disk control remains faster. Physical-budget compliance and scientific novelty against same-factor methods remain open.

These are technical judgments from the current drafts, not estimates of global novelty, customer demand or publication acceptance. A rigorous paper must distinguish the contribution from the strongest applicable existing method. A mathematically correct rearrangement can be useful without being new research.

## Connection To The Founder Objective

[A007](../docs_PRD04/A007-spc-founder-interview-prep-v7.md) makes the important correction: graph memory estimates already exist. The proposed product differentiator is an enforceable workload contract, not the first estimate screen. Its user has an existing graph artifact and a consequential question, especially a dependency/security investigation, and does not trust the run to fit or finish usefully.

The later [whole-workflow research](../research_4gb_20260919/End-To-End-Workflow.md) extends the accounting to source interpretation, preparation, complete delivery, changed data and recovery. Therefore this research optimizes neither a language switch nor the resident algorithm alone.

For the current stringent scenario, use 4,000,000,000 physical bytes and 50,000,000,000 retained prepared bytes. A provisional 3 GB worker allocation is a plan, not measurement. Input, scratch and output staging need explicit allowances; two of the new manuscripts propose an even stricter experiment that caps total live disk at 50 GB too. Those scopes must not be mixed when comparing outcomes.

The seven families come from [Sol-02](../docs_PRD04/Sol-02.md). Public evidence does not establish that these seven have a particular measured adoption share or order. No claim of "85% of Neo4j usage" is used.

## Three Underlying Directions

The [new Boolean PageRank extension](PageRank-Boolean-Two-Membership.md)
adds a separate input contract to A01: each original vertex belongs to at
most two native groups, and sharing both groups still means one edge.
An F-state class stream eliminates as many as Theta(F^2) signature iterates;
it preserves within-class personalization and handles isolates analytically.
The lead replay passed 5,640 exact cases, including the positive grounded
system and rounded-output algebra. Standard block elimination matches the
core, so this is a scoped semantic/representation extension rather than a
claimed new generic solver. The [new streamed experiment](PageRank-Boolean-Streaming-Evidence.md)
now measures Boolean performance separately: 61 tests, corrected P control,
63 certified complete outputs from 81 attempts, roughly 7-36% complete-query
reductions in four constructed cases, ties in two and an 8.34% loss with many
unused factors. Process RSS overlaps. The old weighted approximately-17%
result is neither inherited nor silently redefined. The [actual-byte deflation
follow-up](PageRank-Boolean-Deflation-Implementation.md) now answers that
near-one refusal with a checked publisher and a stronger direct/exact-target
control. All 113 focused tests pass; 180 serial attempts yield 174 full outputs
and six generic refusals. Direct/exact removes class scratch and is fastest
on small/near-one fixtures; direct/generic is fastest on medium/wide ordinary
cases. Deflation restores admission without winning those portfolios. RSS
overlaps. The [heterogeneous-gap extension](PageRank-Boolean-Heterogeneous-Gap.md)
derives a justified bound for unequal pair sizes, checked by 83 exact PSD
cases but not independently reviewed or implemented. Same-source broader
controls, native relevance and physical lifecycle evidence remain.

### 1. Replace A Large State With A Reconstructible Coordinate System

PageRank carries factor coordinates; paths carry entry-gate distances; embeddings carry feature history. Original-node answers are reconstructed with versioned input rows. The common question is not "can we compress the bytes?" but "which independent changing values are necessary for this operator?"

This can preserve distinct node answers without making node rows identical. However, the coordinate system has its own size, conditioning, discovery and invalidation cost. It must remain valid for the exact query, not merely look small in a sample.

### 2. Prove That A Region Of Work Is Unnecessary

Similarity's rank domain proves omitted targets cannot win. Connectivity's surviving witness capacity proves that a smaller current subgraph has the correct partition. Community detection's crossover certificate proves a sequence of future greedy decisions, including when that sequence must stop.

The certificate is not just a cached answer. It specifies the changes and parameter region it survives. Validation traffic and failed-certificate fallback must be paid. A certificate whose validation costs the original job is rarely a product improvement.

### 3. Compute Regular Bulk And Irregular Corrections Separately

Triangles use a known template plus signed defects. Uniform types support type-wide counts and endpoint exceptions; the new cyclic-cover representation needs translated common-neighbor stencils as well, because non-endpoint vertices can change. PageRank's positive defects become additional factors. The distinction between positive rank factors and signed triangle corrections is essential: the PageRank convergence proof cannot silently accept triangle-style signed inputs.

Different operators require different algebras. A Boolean shared-neighbor edge, a shared-neighbor count weight, a shortest-path semiring and a modularity objective are not interchangeable interpretations of the same storage bytes.

## No Generic Neo4j Speedup Table Yet

The historical warm-Bolt result in the previous research concerns three small-output traversal query families. It is not a benchmark of any of these seven new algorithms. Likewise, `16N` bytes of two rank arrays is not Neo4j's total RSS.

The appropriate experimental comparisons are:

1. A competent ordinary operator with the same semantics and full output.
2. The strongest structure-aware competitor, receiving the same source factors or template when those are supplied.
3. An ablation retaining the new storage but not the new state/work elimination.
4. A complete source-to-answer run, then a second query, then changed-data refresh, on the actual constrained machine.

For all seven report preparation time, query time, output time, refresh time, peak physical memory, owned allocations, page-cache treatment, read/write volume, retained and peak live disk, failure/undecided rate and useful completion. A lower kernel state that requires an unavailable builder or misses the decision deadline is not a successful product result.

## What I Would Investigate Next

This is an investigation order, not a request to abandon the seven-family objective.

**Product-oriented first test: A03 paths.** It matches A007's dependency/security wedge and the existing query/driver evidence most closely. Find real tree-dominated input and measure gate growth under ordinary edits. Reject the claimed differentiation if a standard contraction or DAG solver gives the same benefit with less preparation.

Its access-frontier experiment must compare an equally prepared compressed access-pruning baseline. The retained probe changes 32 source answers after one shared route-weight update without rebuilding the indexed source range, but the independent review constructs a factored transit competitor that does the same. A meaningful next experiment needs more general source ledgers and must charge both methods' discovery, updates, rejection and output work.

**Algebra-oriented first test: A01 release.** Its state/contraction theorem and no-expansion kernel survived an independent mathematical challenge. Test natural factorized input, original-residual numerical certification and a strong same-factor solver. A07's current factorized comparator already matches its bounds; seek a stronger mechanism before treating that schedule as an innovation experiment.

**Repeated-query test: A05 similarity.** Compare compact-certificate refusal/fallback cost with complete bucket-index construction and retention. The modeled eight-anchor paid portfolio is about 32.12 GB, but complete old/new replacement is about 64.12 GB and violates the retained 50 GB envelope. A common-feature edit may still touch most targets. Search for actual repeat queries and refresh cadence before choosing either architecture; a synthetic private-feature construction is not a market.

**Research controls for the rest:** measure A04 moving-run length against cached scalar execution from matched starts; compare A01 against published low-rank elimination rather than only CSR; stress A06 exact-zero/near-zero completion; compare A02's now-explicit fragment state against established sensitivity-oracle state and indexing costs.
