## Objective

Determine whether an OSS PostgreSQL or DuckDB extension is the best first adoption path for the bounded graph analytics thesis. Compare other credible first GTM paths, preserve the source-to-answer-to-refresh resource contract, and use explicit next-quarter timelines, adoption experiments and decision rules. Research is not implementation authorization or proof of customer demand.
