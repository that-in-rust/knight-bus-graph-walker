# We Have A Verification Spine, Not Yet Seven Papers

Date: 2026-09-21. This is the current portfolio checkpoint. It consolidates
existing evidence; it does not promote a result or replace a frozen receipt.

## The Answer

We should not describe the work as a complete Neo4j rewrite, seven
publication-ready algorithms, or demonstrated 4 GB graph analytics. We have
instead built a credible research-and-verification spine: exact contracts,
original-score oracles, counterexamples, source receipts, stronger controls,
and documented negative results across all seven families.

One narrow A04 community-comparison candidate is worth further research: an
implicit O(K log K)-work, O(K)-record evaluator for a classical global
fractional b-matching dual. It can prove a constructed decision that our
strongest declared scalar, refinement, meet/join, support and endpoint controls
do not prove. Its components have clear prior art; it has not shown a useful
native workload, physical resource improvement, or a publication-worthy new
theorem. That distinction is the central conclusion.

## Situation, Complication, Question, Answer

Situation: the product thesis is analytical processing with bounded resources:
represent data in the shape of computation, state the RAM/time contract, and
verify exact or explicitly bounded answers.

Complication: most attractive graph optimizations either match a known
primitive, fail against an ordinary control, save a narrow logical cost while
not improving peak state, or lack a realistic source/workflow gate.

Question: after this evidence, what is actually worth building or researching
next?

Answer: preserve the verification infrastructure and focus one bounded,
proof-carrying OLAP slice. The best research bet is the A04 dual only if it
survives a new, frozen, genuinely unresolved candidate/workload study. Product
work should prove end-to-end ingestion, bounded preparation, query execution
and output for one algorithm instead of broad rewrite claims.

## The Portfolio Reached A Useful State In Three Ways

Every family now has stop conditions, rather than a positive claim merely
because it has code, a sketch, an interval or an appealing asymptotic story.

### 2. A04 Delivered A Real Negative Result And A Better Next Object

The initial A04 story was that a bounded quotient summary and joint residual
certificate avoided edge replay while selecting a partition. The precise result
is more useful:

| A04 layer | Verified result | Meaning |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Fixed pair gate | 2,800 differences contained across 140 cases | Comparison arithmetic is sound on this cohort |
| Certify-or-replay executor | 560 policy runs; 20 replay passes / 703,368 logical reads avoided versus caps plus refinement | A certificate can avoid real iterator access within the fixed family |
| Exact-output boundary | Exact scalar score requires 70 replays | Choosing a winner is not returning its exact modularity |
| Stronger-control ablation | Simple signed-support degree control certifies 140/140 too | The row formula and MG cap do not uniquely explain the executor win |
| Global dual candidate | Compact dual oracle gives a strict five-vertex separation | A global certificate can resolve a decision ordinary controls leave open |

The ablation is a success: it prevents a paper claim that a reviewer could
defeat with a short degree argument. The row formula is valid mathematics, but
not the native differentiator.

The replacement research object is:

```text
fixed candidate partitions
        |
        v
support / endpoint / subset controls first
        |
        +-- resolved --> certificate or exact replay output
        |
        +-- unresolved --> fixed-budget implicit global-dual search
                                |
                                +-- resolved --> certificate with rounds charged
                                +-- unresolved --> one exact source replay
```

For a complete-pair residual model with uniform cap D, the dual evaluates

```text
F(y) = sum_i r_i*y_i + D*sum_i<j max(0, c_ij-y_i-y_j)
```

without materializing K choose 2 pairs. Occupied A/B partition cells plus
sorted pairwise-hinge scans calculate its value and a tie-safe subgradient in
O(K log K) arithmetic work and O(K) records for one evaluation. This is a
scoped composition of known duality and known sorted-hinge methods.

On the independent five-node witness, exact residual upper is -1/2. Endpoint
control gives 1; an exact-marginal scalar/meet/join control gives 0. Twelve
bounded heuristic updates find -24847/55440. With the fixture's original-
modularity offset, the scalar control permits A to win while the searched dual
proves it cannot. The fixture separately certifies D=1; it does not infer that
cap from discard-all MG.

The independent review also proves a conservative projected schedule: for
K>=3, an optimum exists in [-K,K]^K with half-integral coordinates. q^2
projected steps give dual gap at most D*K^2*(K-1)/q. The constant is too loose
to market as latency; it supplies a deterministic search-work contract only.

### 3. The Product Thesis Must Be Proven End To End

The portfolio has not disproved the product thesis. It has clarified the
correct first claim:

```text
Do not promise: "we rewrite Neo4j and use less RAM."

Prove: "for one named snapshot workload and one named algorithm, we ingest,
prepare and execute under a declared resource contract, return the same
checked result, and reveal the measured trade-off against Neo4j/GDS."
```

The proof must charge input parsing, validation, preparation time/RAM/disk,
persistent layout, query kernel, output materialization, and refresh/concurrency.
A query-only kernel cannot carry the product proposition.

## What Is Complete Versus Open

Completed infrastructure:

- Seven scoped families have manuscripts, proof attempts, counterexamples,
  primary-source tables and evidence boundaries.
- Multiple results have independent mathematical replays and pinned receipts.
- A04 has exact original-score replay, source-access accounting, negative
  comparator ablations and two independently checked mathematical witnesses.
- The current A04 prototype has ten unit tests; the larger community suite has
  115 tests. The independent dual checker covers 11,718 group cases, 18,940
  partition cases, 200 rational cases, 999 basis cases and eight projected
  schedules.

Still open:

- No family yet has a paper-worthy scientific claim against enough external
  and ordinary controls.
- No workflow demonstrates a physical RAM cap or latency improvement end to
  end on the planned large dataset.
- No result establishes broad Cypher/GDS compatibility, global community
  optimality, dynamic graph support or a full Neo4j replacement.
- The A04 dual has no native workload study. Its strict evidence is constructed
  and its fast search is a heuristic whose useful-workload value is unmeasured.

## The Next Three Concrete Decisions

1. Freeze an A04 dual-workload gate only after defining a broader, summary-
   produced candidate family, output contract and resource accounting. Run
   support/endpoint/subset controls before dual iterations. Exact replay stays
   the fallback. Stop if ordinary controls resolve decisions or total cost wins.

2. Choose one end-to-end OLAP product slice. Build the ingest-to-output harness
   with a hard resource receipt before designing multi-algorithm storage formats.

3. Keep the six other families as evidence-backed option space, not seven
   simultaneous implementation tracks. Allocate next work by measured useful-
   workload gates, not by an algorithm's aesthetic appeal.

## Bottom Line

We have done the intellectual cleanup that makes a serious project possible:
most easy claims were tested hard enough to fail honestly. The next move is
disciplined concentration: either make one OLAP slice resource-receipted end to
end, or make the implicit A04 dual survive a realistic unresolved-decision
gate. Do not combine either bet into a premature complete-Neo4j-rewrite story.
