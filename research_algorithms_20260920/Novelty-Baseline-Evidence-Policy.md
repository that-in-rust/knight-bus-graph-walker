# Novelty Evidence And Comparator Boundaries

Date: 2026-09-20. Methodological correction to the active seven-family research audit. This does not promote any candidate to novel or complete.

## A Mistake To Avoid In Both Directions

An exact implementation of our own proposed algorithm, written again and called a strong comparator, must match it. That is a useful correctness/control result, but not evidence that the algorithm was previously published. Likewise, saying that an incumbent could adopt the optimization is not a priority argument: any published algorithm can in principle be adopted later.

Conversely, putting a new graph-related name on an existing range-minimum query, midpoint-radius encoding or interval intersection does not establish a new algorithm. A constructive reduction to a documented established operation can genuinely narrow the claimed contribution. The strength of that conclusion depends on what the source actually establishes and what work remains in the new composition.

Our research must avoid both errors. The goal is a defensible scientific contribution, not an optimization impossible for anyone else to implement.

## Four Different Comparator Categories

| Category | Evidence required | What matching it establishes |
|---|---|---|
| Published method | Exact source/version, inspected theorem or procedure, compatible input/output model | Can defeat the specific priority or complexity claim if the claimed delta is already present |
| Ordinary instantiation of a published primitive | Explicit reduction, all added preparation/state/work, source for the primitive | Narrows novelty to any nontrivial reduction, composition, contract or implementation result; does not automatically erase all such contributions |
| Independently designed stronger control | Complete procedure and accounting; date/provenance identified | Tests practical or theoretical advantage against that control; its construction in this project is not historical prior art |
| Reimplementation incorporating the candidate's new step | Mark which steps were imported from the candidate | Tests reproducibility and engineering attribution; cannot independently disprove novelty of the imported step |

Equal source data, semantics, budgets and output obligations remain necessary. Do not handicap a published competitor by withholding an input representation that is part of the public comparison contract. But explicitly record when we also give it the newly proposed procedure; information parity and algorithm identity are not the same requirement.

## Consequences For This Portfolio

- **A03 correlated guards:** the exact tail/head query is explicitly reduced to weighted orthogonal range minima with inspected published sources. This genuinely defeats a claim to invent the underlying query primitive. The graph-specific soundness, update contract and useful bounded-bin engineering remain separately evaluated. The constructed per-cell/factored controls are not themselves proof of historical priority for every detail.
- **A06 center/certificate storage:** inspected midpoint-radius prior art supports the existing general principle; the fixed-scale byte-width lemma, lossless graph-output recoding and measured complete-workflow tradeoff are the actual local additions. A second engine using C6 is a codec control, not independent historical evidence. Current results do not establish a paper-worthy graph-algorithm contribution.
- **A07 masked matchings:** voltage closure, interval joins and boundary aggregation have primary precedent. The richer source profile and cyclic root-alignment composition are explicitly derived here. A comparator that executes those same steps demonstrates equivalence to the construction, not that this exact source/compiler/operator contract was already published. A broader prior-art check, nontrivial theoretical delta or compelling empirical contribution is still required.
- **A01/A02/A04/A05 and older A07 variants:** preserve all existing counterexamples and failures against actual published bounds. Re-examine any remaining novelty rejection whose sole argument is that a hypothetical competent solver can copy the candidate. Do not reinterpret such an audit correction as positive novelty evidence.

## The Next Claim Ledger

For the strongest candidate in each family, record one precise claim with:

1. The exact supported input, output, arithmetic and resource contract.
2. The particular new step or theorem, distinguished from inherited components.
3. The nearest inspected published procedure/theorem and the reduction between the two.
4. The strongest additional constructed control, with its construction date and any candidate steps it imports.
5. A separating result or a negative result at the matching scope. A storage-format contribution may need lifecycle measurements; a planning theorem may need a proof and a genuinely new problem-specific characterization.
6. The remaining uncertainty: missing primary text, unimplemented external schedule, artificial workload, weak experiment, or unresolved reduction.

This is not a demand for proof that no related paper exists anywhere. It is a demand for enough precise comparison that a technically informed reviewer can understand what is actually being contributed and challenge it. Nor is it permission to substitute documentation quantity, small test counts or renamed established techniques for the user's seven-algorithm objective.

## Completion Boundary

The full goal remains open. This correction removes an invalid possible rejection criterion; it does not remove the requirement for genuine differentiation. Existing measured regressions, physical-resource gaps and known equivalences remain evidence exactly as recorded. The next research turn should apply this ledger to the strongest outstanding claim, rather than generate another code variant merely because it is easy to verify.
