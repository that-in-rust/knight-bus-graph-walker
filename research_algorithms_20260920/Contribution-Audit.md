# Contribution Audit Against The Original Goal

Date: 2026-09-20; A01 paired evidence updated 2026-09-21. Living audit, not a completion declaration.

## What The User Asked For

The goal is genuine graph-algorithm innovation across seven prioritized families, documented rigorously enough to support serious paper development and directed at real graph-user pains. Seven file names, many passing tiny tests, or seven combinations of existing primitives do not by themselves satisfy that request.

The immediate product pains are resource failures, unpredictable work, expensive preparation/refresh, and difficulty obtaining a useful complete answer on a small machine. The current 4 GB physical / 50 GB retained-prepared scenario is a research envelope, not a general user requirement proven feasible for arbitrary graphs. A paper contribution may be theoretical, empirical, or both; a theoretical result does not license a product speedup/RAM claim.

Interpret comparator-based conclusions using [Novelty Evidence And Comparator Boundaries](Novelty-Baseline-Evidence-Policy.md). Published equivalence, ordinary primitive instantiation, a newly constructed stronger control, and a reimplementation of our own candidate have different evidential force. A competitor being able to adopt a method is not proof that the method was already known. This clarification changes no measured result and promotes no novelty claim.

### New A05 Information-Recovery Result

The [strict-orientation recovery](Similarity-Orientation-Parameter-Recovery.md) now supplies a broader parameterized theorem: numeric Q and I can both grow while only r strict tree splits need branching.
